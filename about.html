behind the camera

hi, i’m Jocelyn.

I’m a Des Moines based photographer drawn to photographs that feel a little imperfect in the best way: soft focus, blown highlights, motion, grain, strange color, real expressions, and small details that would be easy to forget.

I shoot portraits, concerts, city life, and nature. Sometimes the work is loud and chaotic, sometimes it’s quiet and almost still. Either way, I want the final photographs to feel less like a polished record of what happened and more like the way the moment lives in your head afterward.

My approach on a shoot is relaxed and collaborative. I’ll give direction when it helps, but I also leave room for the in-between moments because those are usually the photographs I end up loving most.
